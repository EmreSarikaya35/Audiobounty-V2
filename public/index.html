How to add it to OBS
  1. Click Copy OBS link above. The link is personal, don't share it.
  2. In OBS, click + under Sources and choose Browser.
  3. Paste the link into the URL field and click OK.
  4. Tick Control audio via OBS to see the sound in the OBS mixer.
